# 00 — Overview

## Person

Md Kamal Hosen — Building Management System (BMS) Engineer with 5+ years international
experience. Combines field commissioning (Honeywell Alerton, Tridium Niagara 4, BACnet,
Modbus, HVAC) with software development (React, Next.js, Node.js, IoT/ESP8266/MQTT, Android
apps with 100K+ downloads) and AI-assisted engineering workflow.

## Goals

1. Impress hiring managers at building automation companies and facility management firms.
2. Showcase BMS commissioning experience on large projects (KAFD, Red Palace, commercial
   buildings in Qatar and Saudi Arabia).
3. Demonstrate software development skills as a genuine competence, not a side hobby.
4. Increase interview opportunities from UK and European recruiters.
5. Build a personal brand around "Smart Building Engineer".
6. Allow the owner to add, edit, and delete all content (projects, experience, education,
   blog posts, etc.) from production without touching code or redeploying.

## Target audience

Primary:

- Honeywell, Siemens, Johnson Controls, Schneider Electric
- Facility management companies in UK and Europe
- Technical recruiters (UK, EU, GCC)

Secondary:

- Potential clients/consultancies
- Readers of technical blog content (BACnet, commissioning, BMS)

## Positioning

The unique selling point is the **intersection of two disciplines**:

> *I commission and integrate building automation systems — and I build the software and AI
> tools that make them smarter.*

Most BMS engineers don't ship software. Most developers don't understand HVAC controllers.
Kamal does both. The site makes this single idea visible within 5 seconds of landing.

## Anti-goals (what this site is NOT)

- Not a generic "developer portfolio" template.
- Not flashy, animated, or neon.
- Not a content dump of every job duty ever performed.
- Not a place to disclose confidential employer/client data, salary, IDs, or private code
  (see `09_CONTENT_RULES.md` in ai-context and `09_SECURITY_PRIVACY.md` here).

## Source-of-truth precedence

When the CV (`ai-context/Md_Kamal_Hosen_BMS_Engineer_CV(EU).pdf`) and ai-context disagree:

1. **CV wins** for facts (employers, dates, locations, education, certs).
2. ai-context wins for **tone, philosophy, design rules, content rules**.

Confirmed with the owner in the planning conversation.

## Design north stars

Apple + Stripe + Linear:

- Generous whitespace.
- Tight, confident typography.
- One quiet accent color on an otherwise restricted palette.
- Subtle motion only — page should never feel busy.
- Information density on detail pages (engineers read), restraint on marketing pages
  (recruiters scan).
